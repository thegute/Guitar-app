# Changes waiting for the next build (owner is testing; don't ship until he says "ship it")

(Build 24 shipped everything before this.)

1. Play → chord peek: press and hold a chord name (on the tab or in the Scales & chords strip) → a big chord box (~1.5 in on the inner screen) beside your finger; gone 1.5 s after you let go. Nothing stops.
2. Jump back in (Learn → Today, Songs → My songs): Continue your last song (same bar, loop, speed) or a 15-minute session (today's Zero to Hero routine, short).
3. Songs → Exercises opens with "Suggested for you": a warm-up, what your plan is on, one at your level / a step up.
4. Lick chainer (Learn → Explore, Songs → Exercises, "＋ Add to my lick chain" on every must-know lick): licks as train cars, drag/‹ › to reorder, ×2, "Add one that flows", one key, each lick placed near the last, tempo, space between licks, hear it, play along.
5. Helper knows what's on screen (Play: song, bar, loop, speed, key, chord, hand position, last mistake; Exercises, licks, Jam). Shows "I can see…". Can set your loop and speed ("loop bars 5 to 8 at 70%").
6. In time: a huge 4 3 2 1 count-in over the tab; loop bars shaded with bold start/end lines; "⟲ Again · last time 85%" flash each time the loop goes round; Hear it says "Loading sounds…" while it waits.
7. Gemini: no more long waits / error 503 giving up: faster setting (no "thinking"), tries again, then a lighter Gemini model, then your other free key; gives up after ~45 s.
8. Gemini's voice: Gemini answers are read in a natural Google voice (pick it in Settings → You → AI helper → Gemini's voice, ▶ Hear it). The built-in helper keeps the phone's voice.
9. ✨ bubble fixed: it sometimes closed itself straight after opening (the same tap landed on the dark background). Taps with a little wobble no longer count as a drag.
10. 🎤 Talk (under the ✨ bubble): hands-free. Say "slower", "loop section 5 to 8 and slow the tempo", "full speed", "stop looping", "explain this hand position", "what does an augmented chord do?". Commands happen straight away; the answer shows in a small card and is read out. "Keep talking" = back-and-forth while you play. Your voice isn't judged as wrong notes while you talk.
11. Helper's "app manual": the AI now gets a compact copy of the whole "How to use KGuitar" guide (every page, button, loop/tempo control, lesson and tool) plus "explain theory simply, like a patient teacher", so it can say exactly where things are.
12. Helper sees more: the open theory lesson (title, chapter, what it says) and every Tools page (Tuner: tuning, string, what it hears; Tempo: BPM, time signature, groove, trainer; Chords: chord, notes, the shape shown; Scales: scale, notes, position).
13. Gemini answers stream in word by word in the chat and talk card; Gemini 3 and newer (e.g. 3.8 Flash) run on "low" thinking for speed (retries without it if a model refuses).
