# Pickup Music research (2026-11-07, from its own pages, the Guitar World review, Trustpilot and the App Store listing)
Original summary in our own words; nothing copied. Extend this file when more is learned.

## How it works
- Six grades per pathway. Each grade = daily lessons + play-along exercises + interactive workouts. Pathways by level (Beginner, Late
  Beginner, Intermediate, Late Intermediate) or style (jazz, blues, funk, rock, country, neo soul); short 7/10-day courses; a CAGED pathway.
- A pathway opens by saying what it teaches and how much time it takes. No deadlines: "days" are suggestions; it resumes where you stopped.
- End of a grade: pick a piece from a list, film yourself playing it over a live-recorded backing band ("simulated gig"), a teacher
  reviews the video; passing unlocks the next grade. Weekly live lessons too.
- Extras: 5-minute lessons, essential licks by difficulty/genre, masterclasses, artist lessons, downloadable exercises, Soundslice sheet music.
- About $180/year, 60-day guarantee. iOS 4.9 stars (~1.3K); Trustpilot ~93% five-star (company hasn't recently invited reviews).
- Settings sit behind a profile button on the home screen.

## Why people like it
"Never a doubt what is next"; the jam tracks (most-loved part); CAGED practised not just explained; short 15-30 min days; good for returning players.

## Complaints (our chances)
Hard to know where you are in the structure; early material is old ground for experienced players and the placement quiz may misplace;
no offline; occasional bugs; video player lacks "skip back a few seconds"; no landscape.

## What KGuitar takes (owner OK 2026-11-07)
- 6 grades per pathway, each opening with "what you'll learn + how long", an always-visible "you are here" map, resume.
- Lesson = Learn → Practice → Jam → Check (mic grades it).
- Graduation gig at the end of each grade: pick a piece, play over Band mode, the APP grades it (owner: no sharing needed).
- Placement test to skip ahead. 5-minute lessons + essential licks (ties to Must-know licks). Video "back 5 s".
- Realistic band sound is the make-or-break (their jams are the best-loved part).
