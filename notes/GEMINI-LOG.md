# Gemini week log (newest first)

- 2026-11-15: **Riff Scanner Overhaul — Video On-Screen Tab Scanning, Playhead Masking, Comb-Grid Staff Recovery, Technique Detection & One-Tap Note Fixer**:
  - **Root Causes Diagnosed & Fixed (`src/scan/image.ts`, `src/scan/pictures.ts`, `src/scan/crnnRead.ts`, `src/scan/tabRead.ts`, `src/scan/tabReadSong.ts`, `src/scan/RiffScanner.tsx`, `src/scan/ScanParts.tsx`, `src/scan/ScanPlayer.tsx`, `src/ml/toTab.ts`):**
    1. **Comb-Grid Staff Line Reconstruction (`src/scan/image.ts`, `src/scan/pictures.ts`):** H.264/WebM video compression and dense digit runs frequently washed out 1–2 interior string lines below the strict `0.35` row-contrast threshold, causing `findStrips()` to miss the 6-line tab completely or drop windows. Added `d = [1, 2, 3, 4]` local contrast in `lineAt()`, `findStripsRobust()` contrast-stretched fallback, early horizontal crop-box widening in `Stitcher.add`, and periodic 6-line comb-grid interpolation when `>= n - 2` lines are strong and `onMean - offMean >= 0.17`.
    2. **RGB Chromatic + Frame-Diff Playhead Detection & Stitcher Masking (`src/scan/image.ts`, `src/scan/pictures.ts`):** `playheadX()` previously required a dark vertical line spanning 70% of the entire padded crop height (`g.h`), which failed because `Stitcher.add` pads `2 * gap` above and `3.5 * gap` below the staff (so the 6 strings are only ~48% of `g.h`) and colored playheads (Songsterr green/blue, red/orange/cyan cursors) turn mid-grey in `toGray()`. Preserved per-column RGB chromaticity (`g.chroma`) in `toGray()`, upgraded `playheadX(g, prev)` and `playheadCols(g, prev)` to detect dark, colored, and frame-diff playheads over the staff band (`12%..85%`), masked playhead columns out of `Stitcher.staffed()` so `compose()` automatically fills cursor columns from clean frames, and increased default video frame sampling in `tabFromVideo()` from `0.5–1.5 s` to `0.28–0.5 s` (~2–3.6 fps).
    3. **Visual Twins, Phantom Suppression, Technique Recovery & Stronger Audio Referee (`src/scan/crnnRead.ts`):** Added `VISUAL_TWINS` (`0↔9`, `6↔8`, `3↔8`, `1↔7`, `5↔6`, `11↔14`, `12↔10`, etc.) to `decode()` so blurred fret twins are always evaluated by the Audio Referee (`hear.ts`) and offered in the UI; added `detectStripTechAndClean()` on the straightened 160px strip to remove vertical cursor-bar phantom chords and single-sided tie-curve phantom notes, and to recover slides (`/` and `\`) and hammer-on/pull-off slur arcs (`h` and `p`) between consecutive same-string notes; upgraded `readTabCrnn` to also cross-check moderately confident reads (`p < 0.95`) against the video's audio.
    4. **Interactive One-Tap Note Fixer & Stitched Strip Viewer (`src/scan/ScanPlayer.tsx`, `src/scan/ScanParts.tsx`, `src/scan/RiffScanner.tsx`):** Added `Show scanned strip` in `ScanResult` to inspect the stitched video tab image directly above the interactive tab, wired `from / to` time range inputs into uploaded video scanning (`scanFile`), and added the One-Tap Note Fixer in `ScanPlayer` (`Check N unsure →` or tap any note to audition it, swap to likely fret candidates in one tap, adjust fret `-1/+1` or string `↑/↓`, toggle `h`/`p`/`/`/`\`/`b`/`~`/`x`, or delete a phantom note while keeping the bar's rhythm intact).
    5. **Local-Window Velocity Floor in Sound Transcription (`src/ml/toTab.ts`):** Replaced the global `loudest * 0.22` cutoff in `notesToSong` with a 90th-percentile + 2.5s local-window threshold so a single loud pick pop never erases quieter melody or legato phrases.

- 2026-11-15: **Engine Week Part 4 — Sampler in an AudioWorklet & Per-String Legato (Sound Engine Part 1)**:
  - Added `src/engine/worklets/sampler.worklet.js` (`kg-sampler` `AudioWorkletProcessor`) and `src/engine/sampler.ts` (`prepareStringVoice`, `registerStringVoice`, `legatoOffsetOf`, `ringingDecayAt`, `applySlideAndBend`, `playFingerThud`, `renderSamplerOffline`), wired into `src/audio/pluck.ts`, `src/engine/packs.ts`, and `src/audio/stopAll.ts`.
  - **Per-String Voices:** Playing a new note on a guitar string damps any previously ringing voice on that string over ~12 ms (`tau = 0.004 s`), matching a player lifting or re-picking the string while leaving other strings ringing freely.
  - **Safeguard #4 + Upgrade #7 (Organic Legato on Existing Banks & Packs):** Hammer-ons (`h`) and pull-offs (`p`) start the destination sample ~35 ms past its pick transient (snapped to the nearest zero-crossing via `legatoOffsetOf`), crossfade over ~8 ms from the ringing string, inherit its real-time decay envelope (`ringingDecayAt`) so a hammer-on onto a dying string never jumps back to full pick volume, and add a subtle low-passed (~260 Hz, ~4.5 ms) finger-fret contact thud. Slides (`/` and `\`) ramp `playbackRate` / `detune` smoothly from the ringing string's pitch into the destination sustain body.
  - **Upgrade #6:** Wired `h`, `p`, `/`, `\` techniques and per-string `slideFrom` tracking in `src/audio/tabPlayer.ts`, `src/play/inTime.ts` (guide playback), and `src/screens/PlayScreen.tsx` into `playNote`.
